## 3 likely viewer objections
1. "Moon sandblasting is super slow." -> pre-empted with "slowly".
2. "The dust that lands never glowed, so it isn't a shooting star." -> covered by "the tiniest bits slow down, cool off".
3. "Big meteorites and craters land too, why only dust?" -> covered by "the tiniest bits" + "most are smaller than a grain of sand".
