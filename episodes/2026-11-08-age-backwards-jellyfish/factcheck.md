# Fact-check: "What if you could age backwards?" (2026-11-08)
Independent fact-checker (Sonnet), round 1: **FIX** (3 small edits). Round 2 on the edited script: **PASS**.

## Edits applied
1. "it's only about four and a half millimetres wide" -> "it's at most about four and a half millimetres wide" (adults are at most ~4.5 mm, "roughly the size of a little fingernail": Britannica).
2. "a single jellyfish did it ten times in two years" -> "a single colony did it ten times in a row" (Kyoto Univ.: "ten consecutive rejuvenations" of a colony; the two-year period is only in Gizmodo, so it was dropped).
3. "most get eaten by fish... or killed by disease" -> "many get eaten... or killed by disease" (Britannica lists many predators, not just fish; no percentage exists).
