# Body storyboard: "What if you could age backwards?" (2026-11-08)

Approximate times (final cue times come from the narration's word timestamps, ~60 s narration, ~61 s video). ONE continuous shot, NO cuts (nothing to match, no background swaps); one global camera; the loop seam returns to the frame-0 state.

Set: the template `studio` backdrop (living, soft). On the right stands a big glass AQUARIUM TANK on a low cabinet (x ~560-1000, glass from y ~560 to ~1080), with blue water, a sand floor, a rounded grey ROCK on the sand (centre x ~780), and a little bubbler stone on the sand that sends up a gentle column of bubbles (the bubbles' visible source). Inside floats a big MAGNIFIED cartoon immortal jellyfish (bell with a red-orange inner core, frilly tentacles), pulsing gently. The mascot stands at x ~300 on the studio floor, left of the tank, the whole video.

Premise and the body: the premise ("what if you COULD age backwards") is a question about a real animal that does it. The mascot NEVER ages backwards himself: he is a person looking into a tank. So his body never changes: he stands normally the whole time (no shrinking, no squash/stretch, nothing happens to him). The "if you worked like that" line is shown ONLY inside a small HUD card (a tiny mascot badge that shrinks to a baby badge and grows back, inside the card), clearly a hypothetical picture, never his real body. All transformations happen to the JELLYFISH, inside the tank, and every one is ANIMATED continuously (crumple, sink, sprout, bud, rise, grow), never cut or faded.

Jellyfish states (continuous): MEDUSA (pulsing, frame 0) -> CRUMPLE (tentacles pull in, bell folds into a wrinkled ball, ~0.6 s, fast) -> BLOB sinks onto the rock -> POLYP sprouts up from the blob on the rock (stalk + little crown of tentacles), sways -> BUD: the polyp's tip swells into a tiny bell that pops free -> the baby medusa drifts up, growing to full size at the frame-0 spot; the polyp stalk withers DOWN into the rock (animated shrink to nothing), so at the end the rock is empty exactly like frame 0.
