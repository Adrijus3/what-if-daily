# Fact-check: "What if dinosaurs never went extinct?" (2026-11-07)

Independent fact-checker (Sonnet agent, WebSearch/WebFetch). Round 1 verdict: **FIX** (3 wording overclaims; all numbers passed). All 3 fixes applied verbatim -> **FINAL: PASS**.

## Fixes applied
1. `there'd be no room for big mammals` -> `there'd probably be no room for big mammals`
2. `the strongest of any land animal ever` -> `the strongest of any known land animal`
3. `One group made it through the impact` -> `One dinosaur group made it through the impact`

## Likely objections and pre-emptions
1. "Birds weren't the only survivors" -> covered by fix 3 ("One dinosaur group").
2. "Big mammals might have evolved anyway" -> covered by fix 1 ("probably") plus "Maybe... no you".
3. "Megalodon bit harder" -> "land animal" excludes it; "known" hedges the estimate.

VERDICT: PASS (after fixes)
