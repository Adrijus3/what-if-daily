# Body storyboard: "What if dinosaurs never went extinct?" (2026-11-07)

Approximate times (final cue times come from the narration's word timestamps, ~66 s). ONE continuous shot, NO cuts (nothing to match, no background swaps); one global camera; the loop seam returns to the frame-0 state.

Set: a sunny field (template `field` background, living clouds and grass) with a low grassy HILL across the back right (x 560-1080, top at about y 1010). The mascot stands at x 300 on the ground the whole video. A yellow duck (library `duck`) stands on the grass at x 560, y ~1070, pecking.

Premise and the body: the premise (dinosaurs surviving) never touches the mascot's body. He is a person standing in a field looking at a dinosaur, so he stands normally the whole time: no transformation, no impact near him, nothing hits him. The T. rex is a big friendly cartoon head and neck that RISES UP from BEHIND the hill (its visible source: it was lying behind the hill) and later sinks back down behind it. Its body is hidden by the hill, so its size is believable (an 8 t, ~4 m tall animal behind a hill). Nothing falls in from above the frame; the asteroid appears only inside a HUD "66 MILLION YEARS AGO" panel (a little drawing of Earth with an asteroid flying past/at it), never in the field's sky.
