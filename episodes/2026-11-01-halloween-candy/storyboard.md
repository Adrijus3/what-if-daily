# Body storyboard: "What if you ate all your Halloween candy?" (2026-11-01)

Approximate times (final cue times come from the narration's word timestamps). One mascot rig for the whole video; every cut is a match cut, and both background changes are 0.36 s dissolves.

Premise and the body: eating ~7,000 calories of candy fills the stomach, so his BELLY visibly rounds out (a teal hoodie bulge growing on his tummy, drawn as part of the torso; the rest of his body keeps its normal size and nothing is squashed or stretched). It grows only while candy is actually going in (each gulp adds a little), and it slowly goes back down only while he strolls the calories off. He stays standing the whole time: eating candy doesn't stop you standing.
