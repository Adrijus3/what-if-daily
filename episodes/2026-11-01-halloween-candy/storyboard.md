# Body storyboard: "What if you ate all your Halloween candy?" (2026-11-01)

Approximate times (final cue times come from the narration's word timestamps). One mascot rig for the whole video; every cut is a match cut, and both background changes are 0.36 s dissolves.

Premise and the body: eating ~7,000 calories of candy fills the stomach, so his BELLY visibly rounds out (a teal hoodie bulge growing on his tummy, drawn as part of the torso; the rest of his body keeps its normal size and nothing is squashed or stretched). It grows only while candy is actually going in (each gulp adds a little), and it slowly goes back down only while he strolls the calories off. He stays standing the whole time: eating candy doesn't stop you standing.

## Storyboard check (independent Sonnet checker, 2026-11-01)
Verdict: **FIX** (8 small row edits), all applied above before animating:

2. Teeth row: at the cut no candy mid-air, mouth closed, arms relaxed; tooth/timer/toothbrush clear of the top 230 px and right 160 px, faded out >= 0.3 s before the cut.
3. Road row: bucket, mountain, tooth and HUD belong to the field layer and dissolve with it; characters never faded.
4. Monster row: horn shadow gone before the 37.5 s dissolve; he ends at x 330, hands on hips.
5. Twist row: the brimming bucket arrives with the dissolve; bats/pumpkin glow back to baseline before the loop.
6. Arms: shrug on "myth" held, return to hips >= 0.7 s; comment point is the one gesture, then hold.
7. Eating row: arms hang still, no hand-to-mouth; the candy arc is the single action.
8. Hook: the "all" face is keyed open-eyed so the automatic "seven thousand" reaction lands.
Belly idea: PASS (a local hoodie bulge, not whole-puppet squash; grows only while eating, deflates only while strolling).
